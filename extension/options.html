How to pair

  1. Open the Stash desktop app.
  2. Click the Browser button in the header.
  3. Copy the pairing token shown there.
  4. Paste it above and press Save & Connect.

What gets shared

Window positions, tab URLs and titles, and tab group names — sent only to 127.0.0.1 on your own machine. Incognito windows are never captured.
