What it saves

Window layout

Every window's position and size, scaled to fit whatever monitor you restore on.

VS Code projects

Reopens the project folders you had open, each in its own window.

Terminal folders

Your terminals reopen in the folder they were in, not your home directory.

Browser tabs

With the companion extension, every tab, pinned tab and tab group comes back.

Templates

Set up a group of apps once and launch it whenever you switch tasks.

Entirely local

No account, no cloud, no telemetry. Your workspaces stay on your machine.
